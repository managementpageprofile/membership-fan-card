Frequently Asked Questions

What is a fan card?

A fan card is a membership card that identifies you as a member of a participating celebrity's fan community.

How do I purchase a fan card?

Select your preferred celebrity, choose an available membership option, complete the required information, and follow the payment instructions provided.

Does buying a fan card guarantee a meet and greet?

No. Meet-and-greet opportunities are separate experiences and are subject to availability and confirmation.

Can I request a celebrity who isn't listed?

Yes. You can submit a request through the Request Form.

Are tickets refundable?

Refund eligibility depends on the specific event and applicable cancellation policy.
